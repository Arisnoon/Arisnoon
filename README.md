I'm an AI engineer and NLP researcher. My work sits between research and
systems: evaluating how language models behave, and getting them to run well
in practice.

My NeurIPS 2026 paper,
[*Where You Put the Space Matters*](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/loc/sydney/163674),
studies whitespace robustness in Thai dense retrieval. I also speak about LLM
serving, most recently a Ray Summit 2026 recap on the NVIDIA and vLLM
full-stack collaboration.

Lately I've been building
[a tool-calling agent harness](https://github.com/Arisnoon/fahmai-tool-agent)
for local models and
[an agentic trip planner](https://github.com/Arisnoon/tourism_superai) that
narrates the route as you drive.
